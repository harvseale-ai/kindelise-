How to use Kindlelise

A quick guide to meeting people around shared interests and public plans.

Profile

Add a display name, broad areas, interests and availability. Staff verification is required before discovery, plans and messages become available.

Discover

Use broad-area, interest and Free now filters to find eligible profiles. Exact locations and distances are never shown.

Plans

Create a plan for an established public place, add its public information URL, time and capacity, then open approved plans to join or leave.

Messages

Open an eligible profile and choose Send message. Direct conversations are private to the two participants.

Safety and privacy

Meet only in public places, check plan details before attending, and use the profile actions to block or report privately when needed. Verification is an access gate, not a guarantee of identity or safety.

Premium

Premium expands discovery to nearby broad areas and up to five interest filters. Payment and subscription management use Stripe’s hosted pages.
