The whole journey

Platform overview

Everything is designed to move a small idea towards a real, public meeting without turning it into a large event.

Profile

Add a display name, broad areas, interests and availability. Exact locations are never displayed.

Discover

Find people through shared interests and whether they are open to company.

Plans

Create or join plans at established public places, with capacity and participation kept clear.

Messages

Use direct conversation for the introduction and plan chat after participation is confirmed.

Notifications

See requests, confirmations, plan changes and new activity before arrangements fall apart.

Safety and privacy

Meet in public, check linked details and use private reporting or blocking controls when needed.
